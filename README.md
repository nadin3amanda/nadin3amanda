### Hi, I'm Nadine! Senior Product Designer / UX Lead
I design user-centered, scalable digital products with a strong foundation in frontend development.

I started my career as a freelance web designer, transitioned into frontend development, and eventually moved into UI/UX and product design leadership. That background allows me to design interfaces that are not only visually polished, but also technically feasible, developer-friendly, and production-ready.
